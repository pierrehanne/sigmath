Milo owns 8 marbles and wins 5 more. He could keep counting, but addition tells the story directly: $8 + 5 = 13$.

## Add or take away

**Addition** combines quantities or measures an increase. **Subtraction** finds what remains, what is missing or the difference.

The operations are linked:

$$13 - 5 = 8 \quad\text{because}\quad 8 + 5 = 13$$

In CP, pupils mainly calculate with small numbers. In CE1 and CE2, they add and subtract mentally or in columns, lining up ones below ones and tens below tens.

## Regrouping has meaning

In $28 + 7$, eight ones and seven ones make fifteen ones. Exchange ten ones for one ten: 3 tens and 5 ones make 35.
