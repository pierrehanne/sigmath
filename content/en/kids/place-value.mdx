Write the number thirty-four. The digit 3 does not mean three: to the left of 4, it represents **three tens**.

## Position gives value

Our number system uses ten digits, from 0 to 9. Every place in a number is worth ten times the place to its right.

$$4{,}582 = 4{,}000 + 500 + 80 + 2$$

Zero keeps an empty place: in 3,027, there are no hundreds.

## From CP to CE2

- **CP**: ones and tens, up to 100;
- **CE1**: hundreds, up to 1,000;
- **CE2**: thousands, up to 10,000.
