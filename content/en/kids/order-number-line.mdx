On a number line, moving right reveals larger and larger numbers. This picture helps us compare, order and bracket numbers.

## Compare two numbers

Start with the largest place. In 3,742 and 3,681, the thousands match, so compare the hundreds: 7 hundreds are greater than 6.

$$3{,}742 > 3{,}681$$

The signs open towards the larger number: $8 < 12$ and $12 > 8$.

## Bracket and insert

Bracketing 47 between consecutive tens gives $40 < 47 < 50$. We can insert 48 and 49 between 47 and 50.

Ordinal numbers describe a position: first, second, third… They describe a rank, not a quantity.
