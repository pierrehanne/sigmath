Mental calculation does not mean guessing. Choose a short procedure, explain it, then practise until it becomes familiar.

## Facts to remember

Number bonds to 10, doubles, halves and multiplication tables free your mind for harder problems.

- $7 + 3 = 10$: 3 completes 7 to make 10;
- double 8 is 16; half of 16 is 8;
- $6 \times 4 = 24$ and $24 \div 6 = 4$.

## Decompose to calculate

For $38 + 7$, reach the next ten first: $38 + 2 + 5 = 45$. For $47 - 9$, calculate $47 - 10 + 1 = 38$.

In CE1 and CE2, place value also helps: adding 30 to 246 only changes the tens.
