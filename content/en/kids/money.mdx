To buy an €8 book, you could use a €5 note, a €2 coin and a €1 coin. Different combinations can represent the same value.

## Euros and cents

One euro equals one hundred cents:

$$1\ \text{euro} = 100\ \text{cents}$$

In CP, pupils compose small whole-euro amounts. In CE1, they calculate with euros and cents. In CE2, money introduces decimal notation: €7.35 means 7 euros and 35 cents.

To add €4.80 and €2.50, separate euros and cents. 80 c + 50 c = 130 c, or €1 and 30 c. The total is €7.30.

## Give change

If an item costs €13 and you pay €20, the change is $20 - 13 = 7$ euros. You can also count up from 13 to 20.
