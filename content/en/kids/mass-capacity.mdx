A large empty box can be lighter than a small stone. Size is not enough: a scale compares **masses**.

## Mass and capacity

Mass is measured in grams (g) and kilograms (kg). One kilogram equals 1,000 grams.

Capacity describes how much a container can hold. In CE2, pupils use litres (L), decilitres (dL) and centilitres (cL):

$$1\ \text{L} = 10\ \text{dL} = 100\ \text{cL}$$

Estimate before measuring: does an apple weigh about 150 g or 150 kg? Does a bottle hold about 1 L or 1 cL?

## Exercises

1. Which is heavier: 900 g or 1 kg?
2. A recipe uses 250 g of flour, then 150 g. What is the total mass?
3. How many 20 cL glasses can 1 L fill?
