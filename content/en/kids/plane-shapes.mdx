A shape is not a square simply because it “looks like” one. Its properties, checked with tools, tell us its name.

## Properties tell the story

<ShapeLab />

A square has four equal sides and four right angles. A rectangle has four right angles and equal opposite sides. A rhombus has four equal sides. A right triangle has one right angle.

Draw a circle with compasses: every point is the same distance from the centre. This distance is the radius; a diameter passes through the centre and is twice a radius.

## Construct accurately

A ruler draws and measures segments, a set square checks a right angle, and compasses copy a length and draw a circle. In CE2, pupils also recognise quadrilaterals, pentagons and hexagons.

## Exercises

1. A shape has four equal sides but no right angles. What could it be?
2. A circle has a radius of 3 cm. What is its diameter?
3. Which tool checks whether an angle is a right angle?

<QuickCheck answer="1. A rhombus · 2. 6 cm · 3. A set square" />
