## From survey to chart

In CP, pupils collect data from a small group and make tally marks. They organise results in a table. A bar chart represents each count with a height.

<DataLab />

In CE1, pupils read and build two-way tables and charts whose scale may count in 1s, 2s or 5s. In CE2, they process more data and solve problems using several representations.

<Callout title="Read before answering">
Inspect the title, categories, scale and unit. A bar four squares high may represent 8 people when each square means 2.

## Exercises

1. Votes are 6 for cats, 9 for dogs and 3 for rabbits. Which animal leads?
2. How many people answered altogether?
3. On a chart where one mark means 2 books, a bar reaches 5 marks. How many books is that?
