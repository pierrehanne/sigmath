Two ribbons can be compared by lining up one end. To communicate their length, everyone needs the same unit.

## Measure a length

Place zero at the start of the object, keep the ruler aligned, then read the mark reached by the other end.

<LengthLab />

Choose an appropriate unit: millimetre (mm), centimetre (cm), metre (m) or kilometre (km). During Cycle 2, pupils learn:

$$1\ \text{m} = 100\ \text{cm} \qquad 1\ \text{km} = 1{,}000\ \text{m}$$

Measure a broken line by adding the lengths of its segments. A shape’s perimeter is the length around its boundary.
