A clock shows a moment; a duration measures the time between two moments. A film can start at 2 pm and last 1 hour 30 minutes.

## Read and calculate time

The short hand shows hours and the long hand shows minutes. One full turn of the long hand lasts 60 minutes and moves the short hand forward one hour.

<ClockLab />

Pupils progressively learn the hour, half hour, quarter hour and then the minute. Useful relations are:

$$1\ \text{hour} = 60\ \text{minutes} \qquad 1\ \text{day} = 24\ \text{hours}$$

A calendar locates days, weeks, months and years. A timeline puts events in order.
