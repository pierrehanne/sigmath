Four children receive three biscuits each. Instead of writing $3 + 3 + 3 + 3$, use multiplication: $4 \times 3 = 12$.

## Equal groups

Multiplication finds the total in several equal groups. Turning the groups around does not change the total:

$$4 \times 3 = 3 \times 4$$

Division can mean **sharing** equally or **grouping**. With 14 biscuits for 4 children, each receives 3 and 2 are left.
