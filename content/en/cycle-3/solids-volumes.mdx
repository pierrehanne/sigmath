A cube built from unit cubes measures a new quantity: volume, the space an object occupies.

## Describe solids

Right prisms join the Cycle 2 solids. Cubes, cuboids, pyramids and prisms are described by the shapes and numbers of faces, edges and vertices. A net arranges faces so they fold without overlapping.

In Year 7, one cubic centimetre is the volume of a cube with 1 cm sides. A cuboid 4 cubes long, 3 wide and 2 high contains:

$$4 \times 3 \times 2 = 24\ \text{unit cubes}$$
