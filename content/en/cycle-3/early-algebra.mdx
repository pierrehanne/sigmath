A box hides a number: $2 \times \square + 3 = 17$. We can reason about an unknown before formally using a letter.

## Equality is a balance

The $=$ sign states that both expressions have the same value. Use inverse operations: removing 3 from 17 gives 14, then sharing by 2 gives 7.

Bar models, balances, symbols and growing patterns are pre-algebraic models. They find unknowns and express general rules. The sequence 5, 8, 11, 14… adds 3 each time; term $n$ follows “three times the position, plus two”.
