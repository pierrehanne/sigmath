## Recognise the model

Two quantities are proportional when multiplying one by a number multiplies the other by the same number. Primary pupils reason through multiplicative or additive linearity, without cross-multiplication.

Year 7 formalises proportionality, uses the unitary method and tables with clear quantity names and units. Scales and expressions such as “price per kilogram” extend the model.

On a 1:25,000 map, 1 cm represents 25,000 cm, or 250 m, in reality.

<Callout title="Not everything is proportional">
Age and height are not proportional: at age 20, a person is not twice as tall as at age 10.
