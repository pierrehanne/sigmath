Measuring a boundary means adding all its lengths. The result is a perimeter, always expressed in a length unit.

## Convert and calculate

Metric units change by powers of ten: km, hm, dam, m, dm, cm, mm. We can write 2.35 m as 235 cm.

For a rectangle with length $L$ and width $w$:

$$P = 2 \times (L + w)$$

In Year 7, pupils discover that a circle’s circumference is proportional to its diameter: $C = \pi \times d$, where $\pi \approx 3.14$.

<Callout title="Length or area?">
Perimeter follows the boundary and uses cm or m. Area fills the surface and uses cm² or m².
</Callout>
