A time is a moment; a duration is an amount of elapsed time. Because an hour has 60 minutes, conversions are not base ten.

## Calculate with time

From 9:47 to 11:15, count 13 minutes to 10:00, one hour to 11:00, then 15 minutes. The duration is 1 hour 28 minutes.

Useful units include second, minute, hour, day, year, century and millennium:

$$1\ \text{hour} = 60\ \text{min} \qquad 1\ \text{min} = 60\ \text{s}$$
