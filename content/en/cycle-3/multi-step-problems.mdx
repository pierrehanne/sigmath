A trip costs €8.50 per pupil. Twenty-four pupils attend and an association contributes €45. How much remains to pay? Several operations are needed.

## Understand before calculating

The method remains: understand, model, calculate, answer and regulate. A bar model represents wholes, parts and comparisons without being distracted by keywords.

The total is $24 \times 8.50 = 204$. Then $204 - 45 = 159$ euros remain.
