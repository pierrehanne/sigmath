A statistical investigation does not begin with a graph: it begins with a precise question and a collection method.

## From protocol to interpretation

CM1 collects data and reads tables, bar charts, pie charts and line graphs. CM2 constructs these representations and solves one- or two-step problems.

In Year 7, pupils plan an investigation, take measurements, construct a table and filter data by a criterion. Environmental topics provide real data whose source and quality need discussion.

A chart needs a title, categories, units and scale. A line graph tracks change; a pie chart displays parts of a whole.

<Callout title="Critical thinking">
A graph may be accurate yet misleading when its axis begins at 95 instead of zero. Read the scale before concluding.
</Callout>
