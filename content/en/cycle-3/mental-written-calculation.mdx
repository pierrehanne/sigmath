A good calculator does not write every operation: they estimate, choose a strategy, calculate and check.

## Choose a method

For $49 \times 6$, use distribution: $(50 - 1) \times 6 = 300 - 6 = 294$. To multiply by 5, multiply by 10 and halve.

Written methods remain useful for awkward numbers. Cycle 3 consolidates addition, subtraction, multiplication and whole-number or decimal division. Year 7 also multiplies two decimals.

$$2.4 \times 1.5 = 3.6$$

An estimate checks the decimal point: $2.4 \times 1.5$ is close to $2 \times 2$, so 36 is impossible.

## Brackets and priority

In $7 + 3 \times 4$, multiply first. Brackets change the order: $(7 + 3) \times 4 = 40$.
