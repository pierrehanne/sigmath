Two rectangles may have equal perimeters without covering equal surfaces. Area measures the space inside the boundary.

## Count, then calculate

A square with 1 cm sides defines 1 cm². Counting squares on a grid builds the formula’s meaning:

$$A_{rectangle} = L \times w \qquad A_{square} = s \times s$$

CM1 compares and measures area by tiling. CM2 uses square and rectangle formulas. Year 7 converts units such as $1\ \text{m}^2 = 100\ \text{dm}^2$ and $1\ \text{dm}^2 = 100\ \text{cm}^2$.

<Callout title="Why 100?">
A 1 m square contains 10 rows of ten 1 dm² squares: that is 100 dm², not 10.
