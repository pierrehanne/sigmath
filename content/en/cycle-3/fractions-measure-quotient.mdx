Three baguettes shared equally between four people give exactly $\frac{3}{4}$ of a baguette each. Here the fraction expresses a quotient.

## Several meanings, one number

A fraction can be part of a whole, a measure, an operator or the exact result of a division. Thus $\frac{7}{4}$ lies beyond 1 and equals $1 + \frac{3}{4}$.

Calculations sometimes need a common denominator:

$$\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$$
