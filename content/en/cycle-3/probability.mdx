When a fair die is rolled, each face has one chance in six. The previous result does not change the next: the die has no memory.

## From possible to probable

CM1 classifies events as impossible, possible or certain and compares likelihoods. CM2 counts equally likely outcomes and says “a chances out of b”.

<ProbabilityLab />

In Year 7, probability is a number from 0 to 1, expressible as a fraction, decimal or percentage. Three of six die faces are even:

$$\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} = 0.5 = 50\,\%$$

Observed frequency after many rolls often approaches theoretical probability without always equalling it.
