## Read large place-value groups

Digits are grouped in threes: units, thousands, millions and billions. In 327,045,608, digit 7 means 7 million while digit 5 means 5 thousand.

CM1 reaches 999,999; CM2 reaches 999,999,999; Year 7 introduces one billion. To compare, inspect the number of digits, then the first different place.

## Multiples and factors

A number is divisible by 2 when its units digit is even, by 5 when it ends in 0 or 5, and by 10 when it ends in 0. The factors of 18 are 1, 2, 3, 6, 9 and 18.
