## Move and see in space

CM1 and CM2 pupils produce instruction sequences for routes through a town or grid. They solve cube-arrangement problems and move between three-dimensional objects and flat representations.

In Year 7, spatial visualisation becomes central: count cubes, including hidden ones, and match several views to one arrangement.

<Callout title="Hidden cubes count">
If a visible cube sits on the second level, another cube must usually support it below, even when the drawing hides it.
</Callout>

## Exercises

1. A tower shows 4 square faces stacked vertically. What is the minimum number of cubes?
2. A top view shows 6 occupied squares, each with height 2. How many cubes?
3. A robot faces east, turns a quarter-turn left, then moves. Which direction?

<QuickCheck answer="1. 4 cubes · 2. 12 cubes · 3. North" />
