A 75 cL bottle holds the same amount as 0.75 L. Changing unit does not change the measured quantity.

## Mass and capacity

Mass units include tonne, kilogram, gram and milligram. Capacity units include litre, decilitre, centilitre and millilitre.

$$1\ \text{kg} = 1{,}000\ \text{g} \qquad 1\ \text{L} = 1{,}000\ \text{mL}$$

CM1 consolidates comparisons, conversions and problems. These measures naturally use decimals: 1 kg 250 g is 1.25 kg.

<Callout title="Estimate first">
A car weighs roughly one tonne and a chocolate bar roughly 100 g. An order of magnitude catches unsuitable units.
</Callout>
