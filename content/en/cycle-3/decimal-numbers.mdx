Infinitely many numbers lie between 2 and 3. Tenths, hundredths and thousandths locate many of them precisely.

## From fraction to decimal

$4.107$ encodes $4 + \frac{1}{10} + \frac{7}{1000}$. The zero keeps the hundredths place: 4.107 is not 4.17.
