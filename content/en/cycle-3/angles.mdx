Side length does not change an angle’s opening. Its measure depends only on the rotation between two rays.

## Compare, name and measure

CM1 compares angles and recognises right angles. CM2 uses a protractor to measure degrees. Year 7 studies zero, acute, right, obtuse, straight and full angles, as well as adjacent and supplementary angles.

<AngleLab />

An acute angle is below 90°, a right angle is 90°, an obtuse angle lies between 90° and 180°, and a straight angle is 180°. An angle bisector creates two equal angles.

Angles in a triangle sum to 180°. In a right triangle, the two acute angles therefore sum to 90°.
