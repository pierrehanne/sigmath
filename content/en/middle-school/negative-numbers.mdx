In the morning, it is $-3°C$. At noon, the thermometer reads $5°C$. The temperature crossed zero, but the numbers keep going on either side.

## An endless line

On a number line, values increase as you move right. That means $-2$ is greater than $-7$, even though $7$ is greater than $2$.

$$-7 < -2 < 0 < 5$$

## Measuring change

Moving from $-3°C$ to $5°C$ is a rise of $8°C$: first three degrees to reach zero, then five more.

If it is $2°C$ and the temperature drops by $6°C$, what is the new temperature?
