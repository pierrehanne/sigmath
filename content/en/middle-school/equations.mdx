An equation works like a balance: whatever is on the left of the equals sign weighs exactly as much as whatever is on the right.

## Keep the balance

To solve $x + 4 = 11$, subtract $4$ from both sides. The balance stays level:

$$x + 4 - 4 = 11 - 4$$

So $x = 7$. The essential rule is simple: **do the same operation to both sides**.

<Callout title="A useful habit">
Put your answer back into the original equation. If both sides are equal, your solution is correct.
</Callout>
