A die rolls across the table. Nobody knows the result in advance, but outcomes are not completely mysterious: we can measure how likely they are.

## Favourable outcomes, possible outcomes

A fair die has six possible outcomes. Only one shows a $4$, so the probability of rolling $4$ is:

$$P(4) = \frac{1}{6}$$

For an even number, three outcomes work: $2$, $4$, and $6$. So $P(\text{even}) = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$.

<Callout title="Between impossible and certain">
A probability is always between $0$ and $1$. Zero means impossible; one means certain.
</Callout>
