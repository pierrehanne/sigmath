Look at a bridge, a crane, or a roof frame: triangles are everywhere. Unlike a quadrilateral, a triangle cannot change shape unless the length of one of its sides changes.

## Three sides, several families

An **equilateral** triangle has three equal sides. An **isosceles** triangle has two. A **right** triangle contains a $90°$ angle.

The three angles inside any triangle always add up to:

$$\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 180°$$

<Callout title="Thought experiment">
Cut the three corners from a paper triangle and line them up. Together, they make a straight angle.
</Callout>
