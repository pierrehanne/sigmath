# Cycle 3 curriculum coverage

Source: `data/cycle-3.pdf` — *Programme de mathématiques pour le cycle 3*.

The pathway follows the progression CM1 → CM2 → 6e while ordering ideas by prerequisite. Every lesson includes a concrete introduction, explicit progression and corrected exercises.

| Calcul mental et quatre opérations | 04 Mental and written calculation |
| Résolution de problèmes | 05 Multi-step problems |
| Algèbre | 06 Reasoning with an unknown |
| Longueurs | 07 Lengths and perimeters |
| Masses et contenances | 08 Mass and capacity |
| Aires | 09 Measuring and calculating area |
| Angles | 10 Comparing and measuring angles |
| Temps et durées | 11 Calculating times and durations |

| Probabilités | 16 Discovering probability |

| Pensée informatique | 18 Introducing computational thinking |

Interactive representations cover the fraction number line, decimal hundredths, unknown-value balance, area/perimeter, angle measurement, repeated random experiments, proportional recipes and instruction sequences.
