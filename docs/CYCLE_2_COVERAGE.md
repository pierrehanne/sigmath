# Cycle 2 curriculum coverage

Source: `data/cycle-2.pdf` — *Programme de mathématiques du cycle 2*.

The website orders the curriculum from the most concrete prerequisite to later applications. Each lesson includes the CP–CE1–CE2 progression, worked explanations and three corrected exercises.

| Official programme area | Website lessons |
| --- | --- |
| Nombres entiers | 01 Counting collections; 02 Place value; 03 Comparing and ordering |
| Four operations | 04 Addition and subtraction; 05 Multiplying, sharing and grouping |
| Calcul mental | 06 Mental calculation strategies |
| Résolution de problèmes | 07 Solving a word problem |
| Fractions | 08 Discovering fractions |
| Longueurs | 09 Measuring lengths |
| Masses et contenances | 10 Mass and capacity |
| Monnaie | 11 Calculating with money |
| Temps et durées | 12 Time and duration |
| Solides | 13 Exploring solids |
| Géométrie plane | 14 Shapes and their properties |
| Repérage dans l’espace et symétrie | 15 Position, movement and symmetry |
| Organisation et gestion de données | 16 Tables and bar charts |

Interactive representations are used where manipulation clarifies the idea: grouping into tens, equal sharing, fractions, measuring length, elapsed time, shape properties and bar charts.

Cycle 3 is intentionally not included in this batch.
